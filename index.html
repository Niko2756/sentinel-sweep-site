Simple pricing

Free to scan. One-time unlock for transfers.

Sentinel Sweep is free to download. Scan, review, and prepare a transfer queue for free. A one-time Transfer Access purchase unlocks Quick Copy, Quick Move, the final transfer review, and all new Copy and Move transfers.

Safety controls stay available. Pause, cancel, interrupted-transfer recovery, and eligible Undo remain available for transfers already underway. Purchasing access never starts a file operation automatically.

Transfer Access uses Apple StoreKit. In StoreKit-enabled TestFlight builds, purchases use Apple's sandbox: testers are not charged, and test purchases do not carry over to the production Mac App Store version.

Privacy by design

Your files stay under your control.

The app reads the files and folders you choose so it can classify media, organize a review, and—when Transfer Access is active—carry out the file actions you request.

  • No Sentinel Sweep account is required. Optional App Store purchases use your separate Apple Account.
  • No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs are included.
  • No app feature uploads your scanned media, filenames, file paths, scan results, learned decisions, or transfer logs to an online service.
  • Saved scan and transfer state remains on your Mac and can be removed with in-app controls.

This public website and the support mailbox are separate services. GitHub may process basic visitor logs for Pages, and Google processes messages you choose to email. Neither service participates in Sentinel Sweep's in-app scanning.
