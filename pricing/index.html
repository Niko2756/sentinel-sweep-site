A deliberate handoff

See the plan before you pay.

You can reach the decision point without moving a file. Sentinel Sweep keeps purchase confirmation separate from the file action itself.

  1. 1
    Scan and review for free

    Choose sources, inspect local results, filter them, and make the final classification decisions.

  2. 2
    Prepare the transfer

    Build a queue, choose destinations, select Copy or Move, and inspect the complete preflight.

  3. 3
    Unlock through Apple

    If Transfer Access is not active, the app will present Apple's one-time in-app purchase flow and localized price.

  4. 4
    Start deliberately

    After access is confirmed, the preflight will stay open. You choose Start Copy or Start Move; the purchase never starts it for you.

Safety, restoration, and refunds

Safety controls are not held behind the purchase screen

Pause, cancel, interrupted-transfer recovery, and eligible Undo remain available for transfers already underway. This prevents a later purchase-state problem from taking away controls needed to protect your files.

Restore Purchases

After Transfer Access becomes available, Sentinel Sweep will provide a user-invoked Restore Purchases control. Use it if an eligible purchase is not recognized after a reinstall or on another compatible Mac. Apple may ask you to authenticate with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Refunds and revocations

Apple processes App Store payments and decides refund eligibility. A refunded or revoked Transfer Access purchase returns the app to its free feature set for starting new transfers. To request an eligible refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

TestFlight purchases

When in-app purchase testing is enabled in a TestFlight build, Apple uses its sandbox. Test purchases cost testers nothing and do not carry over to the production Mac App Store version.

No App Store button yet

Sentinel Sweep does not yet have a public Mac App Store download URL. This page will link to Apple only after the listing and Transfer Access purchase are ready.
