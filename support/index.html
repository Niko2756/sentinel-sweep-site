Support

Questions, beta feedback, and problem reports go directly to the dedicated Sentinel Sweep support mailbox.

Publisher: Niko Lim For Sentinel Sweep on macOS

Start here

Email SentinelSweepSupport@gmail.com. During the beta, messages are reviewed by the publisher and response times may vary.

Send a useful report

A short, specific report makes it much easier to reproduce a problem. Please include:

  • your macOS version and Mac model;
  • the Sentinel Sweep version and build number shown in the app;
  • the screen you were using, such as Setup, Review, Transfers, History, or Settings;
  • the steps immediately before the problem;
  • what you expected and what happened instead; and
  • the exact text of any error message.

A screenshot can help, but first hide or crop any private filenames, file paths, thumbnails, account names, or other personal information.

Do not email private media or secrets

Please do not attach the files you scanned, passwords, passkeys, signing keys, recovery codes, security-scoped bookmark data, or unredacted logs. A description of the file type and the visible error is usually enough.

Common help

I cannot choose or reopen a file, folder, or drive

Use the folder button on Setup to choose the item again. Sentinel Sweep can remember access to items you approve using macOS security-scoped bookmarks. If a removable volume was renamed, reformatted, disconnected, or mounted differently, reselecting it may be necessary.

A scan system says it is unavailable

Open the Scan Systems area on Setup and note the exact status shown for Media Analysis, Names, Video Frames, or Results. Include that wording in your support email. Availability can depend on the media type, the macOS frameworks present on the Mac, and the bundled decoder.

A result looks incorrectly classified

Sentinel Sweep provides classifications to help with review; it does not replace your judgment. Use the Review controls to change the decision. If you report a false positive or false negative, describe the general file type and the signal shown in the app. Do not send the media itself.

A copy or move did not finish as expected

Check Transfers and History for the status recorded by Sentinel Sweep. Before retrying, confirm that the source and destination are connected and have enough free space. If you chose Move, remember that a successful move removes the original only after the destination copy has been placed. If you chose Overwrite, an existing destination item may be replaced.

I want to reset local app data

Sentinel Sweep separates learned memory, transfer history, current results, checkpoints, and full factory reset so you can remove only what you intend. See Privacy Choices before using Restore Factory Defaults; the complete reset is scheduled and finishes the next time the app launches.

Product feedback

Ideas about the review workflow, accessibility, performance, removable-drive behavior, and confusing wording are welcome. Put “Feedback” in the subject line and explain the outcome you wanted to achieve.

Privacy questions

The same mailbox accepts privacy questions and requests concerning an email you sent to support. The app itself does not create an account or upload scan data to an online service. Read the Privacy Policy and Privacy Choices for details.
